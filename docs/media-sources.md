## Processing applied

Website derivatives were created from the source files by:
- EXIF orientation normalization
- crop-to-cover resizing
- saturation and contrast reduction
- warm ivory color wash
- WebP compression
- metadata-free output from Pillow

Current derivative sizes are intentionally small; photography remains secondary to Nolan typography, SVG diagrams and modular interaction.

## Rights policy

Do not infer that a permissive stock-photo license clears every third-party right visible in an image. Avoid future assets containing prominent trademarks, identifiable private artwork, or people where releases or endorsement implications may matter.
